01 / About

I'm a developer who became a founder. I build custom platforms, WordPress sites and online stores, and I make sure they get found and they sell. Since 2024 I've been leading Iijad, helping businesses across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf grow online.

I started as a backend developer in 2018 with PHP and Laravel, then added WordPress and SEO, because a website that nobody finds, or that doesn't sell, isn't really finished. Today I lead Iijad's team and strategy, and I still stay close to the code.
